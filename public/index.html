How to play

  • Innocent: hide, survive, grab coins. If the Sheriff dies, pick up the gun and become the Hero.
  • Sheriff: find and shoot the Murderer. Shoot an innocent and you die too.
  • Murderer: kill everyone. Knife out means people can see you, so be sneaky.

Controls

  • WASD move · Mouse aim
  • Left click stab / shoot
  • Q or tap Right click throw knife
  • 3D: hold Right mouse + drag to turn the camera · Scroll to zoom
  • Shift 🔒 Shift Lock: the mouse turns the camera and you face where you look
  • E or 1 pull out / put away weapon
  • F 💨 juke (quick dash) · Space jump · B 💣 bomb jump (way up) · hold C crouch
  • Dead? Click to switch who you spectate
  • 📱 Phone: drag on the left to move, tap anywhere else to aim and attack
