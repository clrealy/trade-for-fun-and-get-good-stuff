What we collect

  • Your email address and password, when you make an account. These are handled by Google Firebase Authentication. The game never sees your password, and Firebase stores it hashed.
  • Your username, which other players see in rounds and on trades.
  • Your game progress: coins, items, level, stats, trophies and redeemed codes, stored in Google Cloud Firestore.
  • In-round chat messages are sent to the players in your round. They are not saved.

We don't collect your location, contacts, photos or device identifiers. We don't show ads and we don't sell or share your data with anyone. The only other company involved is Google, which hosts the accounts and database for us.

How it's protected

All traffic is encrypted (HTTPS and secure WebSockets). Only the game server can change your profile, so other players can't edit your coins or items.

Deleting your account

In the game, open the Play tab, scroll to Account and tap Delete my account. This permanently deletes your login, username and all your progress right away. If you can't sign in, email us at the address below and we'll delete it within 30 days.

Kids

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect data from them. If you think a child under 13 made an account, contact us and we'll delete it.
